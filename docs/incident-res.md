# Northwind Tech — Incident Response Policy (v3.0)

## 1. Detection and response

### IR-1.1 Triage target

Security incidents are triaged within 1 hour of detection. A 24/7 on-call rotation covers all production systems.

## 2. Communication

### IR-2.1 Customer notification

Affected customers are notified within 72 hours of Northwind confirming a breach involving their data.

## 3. Review

### IR-3.1 Post-incident review

A post-incident review is completed within 10 business days, with a written summary of root cause and corrective actions.
