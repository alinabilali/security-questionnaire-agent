# Northwind Tech — Access Control Policy (v2.3)

## 1. Authentication

### AC-1.1 Multi-factor authentication

All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) on every corporate system, including email, source control and the cloud console. Approved factors are authenticator apps and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not permitted.

### AC-1.2 Password requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are checked against a list of known breached passwords. A password manager is provided to all staff.

## 2. Access reviews

### AC-2.1 Quarterly access reviews

User access to production systems and customer data is reviewed every quarter by system owners. Accounts with no login for 60 days are disabled automatically.

### AC-2.2 Least privilege

Access is granted by role under the principle of least privilege. Administrator rights require written approval from the employee's manager and the security team.

## 3. Offboarding

### AC-3.1 Access removal

Access to all systems is disabled within 24 hours of an employee's last working day.

### AC-3.2 Device return

Company laptops and phones must be returned on departure and are remotely wiped.

### AC-3.3 Credential rotation

Any shared credentials known to the departing employee are rotated within 7 days.

## 4. Personnel screening

### AC-4.1 Background checks

Background checks are conducted for employees who will have access to production systems or customer data. Other roles are not subject to background checks.
