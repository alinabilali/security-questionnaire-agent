# Northwind Tech — Data Protection and Retention Policy (v2.1)

## 1. Encryption

### DP-1.1 Encryption at rest

Production databases, backups and employee laptops (full-disk) are encrypted at rest using AES-256.

### DP-1.2 Encryption in transit

All external and internal network traffic uses TLS 1.2 or higher.

## 2. Retention

### DP-2.1 Customer data after contract end

Customer data is deleted within 30 days of contract termination. Deletion is confirmed in writing on request.

### DP-2.2 Log retention

Audit and access logs are retained for 12 months.
